# Daily assistant prompts

Open **To-do list → Your daily assistant** to configure three compact additions to the Actions receipt. Home shows the same guidance from saved data without a new calendar or bank lookup.

- **Do first:** choose up to three pending tasks for today, or expand the tomorrow plan before the scheduled morning print. Today's and tomorrow's choices are separate. The receipt uses the matching UK date and omits completed, skipped, deleted or not-yet-due tasks. Choices do not carry forward indefinitely or automatically change task priority. Clear the selection to remove the section. Highlighted tasks appear once on paper; the full underlying task text still drives existing finance/shopping triggers.
- **Next appointment:** the next timed calendar event that starts today at or after receipt generation. Past, ongoing and all-day events remain in the regular calendar rather than being described as the next appointment. Add preparation using the exact calendar title (case-insensitive). Rules apply to future occurrences with that title. Enter an optional 1–240 minute travel/arrival allowance to show a leave-by date/time; no journey duration or route is inferred. Include any desired arrival buffer in the allowance. Leave-by calculations use elapsed minutes across UK clock changes, and an already-passed leave time is labelled. Leave the travel field blank for online appointments or when no estimate is known.
- **Before bed:** up to three tomorrow appointments before noon, their preparation checklists and up to five personal instructions. Additional early appointments are explicitly signposted to the calendar. Personal instructions repeat until edited or removed. Bin reminders sit in this section, with **PUT BINS OUT TODAY** and the actual collection date, and are printed only once. Existing failed/stale council warnings remain visible.

Appointment preparation and personal bedtime notes can be edited or removed on the task page. Reusing an exact title replaces its rule. Each checklist allows five single-line instructions of up to 160 characters. All editing requires login and CSRF protection; rejected preparation keeps the draft and leaves saved data intact.

This adds no provider retrieval or automatic task completion. The live receipt uses its existing calendar collection; saved Home guidance explicitly shows when the calendar was checked. Cached calendar data retains existing receipt freshness labels; a failed collection does not manufacture appointments. Settings are stored alongside tasks in `data/daily_lists.json`, included in private backups and update checkpoints, and checked by the existing restore validator. Changing priorities or preparation flags a previously generated preview as using older settings. Older task stores without assistant settings remain valid.
