# Daily finance clarity and paper use

## Finance charts

Main Finance offers local 30- and 90-day bank-cash charts: no future income, expected non-salary income when configured, and the protected buffer. Incoming dates and bills of £100 or more are marked. Invalid/incomplete baseline data withholds numeric charts.

After generating Test finance, Explore the figures includes:

- Bank cash and protected reserves over the selected forecast.
- Savings account paths, goals and contribution caps. Opening balances, actual period allocations, scheduled contributions and AER assumptions are reused. The endpoint is the authoritative forecast total; no assumed investment return is added.
- Amex with the selected plan versus scheduled repayments alone, using the same APR assumption.
- A what-if cash comparison for lower/higher spending, one extra expense and a missed first salary. The extra expense uses the existing comparison amount; missed salary resumes at the next scheduled payday. Exact goal/clearance dates accompany the stress cases.
- A first-period allocation bar. Scheduled savings and direct debits are already inside scheduled payments; reserve transfers are not new income. Spare starting cash is included where selected.

Lines can be toggled with labelled checkboxes. Each chart has exact values in expandable tables and works without external chart services. Graph data is saved with the generated test receipt, so reopening it does not fetch bank data or rerun calculations. Old archives without chart data remain readable.

## Accounts and changes

The savings summary lists accounts without requiring a target or valuation. Missing values are labelled and remain excluded from numeric allocation. Targets are optional milestones; maximum balances stop new contributions. Premium Bonds retain the £50,000 cap.

Edit account & planning settings opens one form for display name, an optional new dated valuation, account type, target, maximum, contribution share and eligible cash-interest assumptions. Canonical identity and contribution records remain linked. ISA subscriptions are recorded separately. Validation occurs before writes; history is restored if saving settings fails.

What changed compares two complete successful finance checks, not page views. It reports cash, Amex, dated savings balances and new/changed upcoming payments. Changes in a savings valuation are not labelled investment gains. Bank source changes are labelled as not directly comparable. Only two snapshots are retained privately and included in backups.

## Today and explanations

Needs your attention today ranks bin preparation, near-term/overdue MOTs, today's birthdays, the next saved appointment, fresh cash-shortfall warnings and chosen/due tasks. Each item explains its date or priority. MOT booking tasks are not duplicated in this ranked list. All dashboard information comes from local records; browsing does not request bank data.

Prepare print explains why sections and date-triggered reminders appear. These explanations remain on the web to avoid repeating them on daily paper.

## Paper saving

New installations default to daily summary; an existing explicit detail preference is preserved. Paper-saving spacing is enabled by default, including when older settings have no spacing preference. It can be disabled under Receipt options. It removes repeated blank lines, adjacent repeated rules and blank lines between news stories, without removing headlines, warning text, amounts, tasks, weather graphs or illustrations. The captured preview and physical printer receive the same shortened text.

Finance also omits a second, identical accessible-savings runway when accessible savings are zero, and repeats each identical projection warning only once. Daily summary caps headlines at two per news section; full detail remains available per generation or print without changing saved preferences.

Preview and Prepare print estimate length from saved text and image dimensions. The estimate assumes 42 columns, 3.75 mm text line pitch, 203-dpi images and 15 mm cutter feed per page. It is approximate: actual printer spacing, wrapping and newly collected content can differ. Annual use assumes this same receipt length every day.

A representative three-section news fixture with three headlines per section saves six blank lines (about 2.25 cm at the assumed pitch) without dropping any headline. Actual savings depend on the day's content. Existing weather/receipt visual references remain unchanged.
