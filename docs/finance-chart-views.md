# Cash forecast chart

Bank cash charts offer **Cash-flow samples** and **Monthly low / high range**.
Forecasts longer than six months open in the monthly view. Ranges come from the
actual daily cash ledger: they describe variation within each month, not forecast
uncertainty. Cash-flow samples retain the familiar dated forecast points.

Regular £ gridlines, month/year labels and a protected-reserve line provide scale.
The whole-forecast summary shows the exact daily low, its date, the protected floor
and the closing balance. The first 3/6/12 calendar month selector changes only the
plot, leaving that summary unchanged. Hover, tap or focus the chart and use
Left/Right/Home/End to inspect values. Legend checkboxes hide individual series;
the exact-values table provides a readable alternative and works without JavaScript.
