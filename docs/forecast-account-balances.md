# Test forecast balances and savings overflow

The test form defaults the first payday/lump-sum receipt date to today's London date each time it opens, including when old results or presets are loaded. Change the date before generating a forecast to model another payday. Generated and saved results retain the date actually submitted.

Balance goals are milestones and do not stop contributions. Optional maximum balances redirect extra savings to other eligible accounts using equal allocation or selected shares. Known-balance accounts without a maximum can continue receiving contributions, including after their goal is reached. Premium Bonds have a hard £50,000 holding cap, including scheduled contributions; room is reserved for scheduled payments so proposed extra transfers cannot use it twice. Regular contributions to other accounts, such as Hargreaves Lansdown, continue independently of their optional balance goals.

Amounts with no eligible destination stay in projected bank cash rather than becoming unnamed savings transfers. The selected repayment strategy still applies: Amex first prioritises debt; savings first can use released savings capacity for debt before leaving the remainder in bank. Reaching an overall savings milestone does not stop the forecast. Savings targets and bank runway use the same actual planned transfers, so account totals and remaining cash agree.

Monzo's target defaults to £500 on the test form and is editable. Its starting balance is optional; otherwise the bank snapshot is used, or zero for a wholly manual combined cash scenario. The starting Monzo balance is included in combined bank cash, not added to it. With a Monzo target, the forecast protects at least £1,000 for HSBC plus Monzo's target and any other protected reserves. An internal Monzo top-up uses this reserved bank cash; it is not a second deduction or an investment.

The receipt includes a compact end-of-forecast balance list: HSBC, Monzo, Amex debt and each recorded savings/investment account. Unknown account balances remain labelled unavailable. If forecast bank funds cannot cover the Monzo target it is labelled unfunded. The separate bank reserve and savings balances are hypothetical allocations; no money is moved. Income, bills and spending are modelled through HSBC while Monzo is held at its target.
