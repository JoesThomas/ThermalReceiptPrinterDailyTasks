# Recording an instalment as settled

In **Accounts → Instalments**, use **Mark as settled** after the provider confirms
payment in full. The date defaults to today and can be changed to a past date.
The record and payment history remain saved locally; the remaining balance and
payment count become zero, excluding the plan from new forecasts. Regenerate any
existing Finance/test receipt to see the change. Archived forecasts remain as saved.

When a unique saved monthly commitment matches the plan's name and amount, a
checkbox lets you stop that entry in future forecasts too. Review the match before
saving. Ambiguous or generic Amazon entries need manual review under Monthly
commitments. Bank transactions remain unchanged. No money is sent and no bank
Direct Debit is cancelled by this button.

**Reopen this instalment** restores the balance and schedule recorded before
settlement and reactivates the linked entry. Use this before correcting a settled
plan. Confirmations from stale pages are rejected so that they cannot settle a
record that has since changed. Settlement data is included in private backups.
