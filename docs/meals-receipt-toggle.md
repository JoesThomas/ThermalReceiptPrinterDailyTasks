# Meals on receipt

Open **Settings → Daily receipt**, untick **Meals on receipt**, and save settings. Meals default to enabled for existing installations. Older settings forms without the new toggle retain the current preference.

When disabled, newly generated receipts omit the food page, its recipe/preparation/shopping-plan output, meal generation and its paper cut. The print-plan checkbox and food-only preview option are disabled or omitted. Explicit food-only generation requires enabling the setting again; multi-page generation leaves the other requested pages intact. Preview page labels and captures still identify finance correctly when food is omitted.

Saved recipes, plans, meal confirmations and receipt archives remain intact. The interactive meal planner and explicit archived reprints remain available. Shopping-list commands on the Actions receipt are independent of the meal-planning page. Re-enable the toggle to restore the usual food page, then generate a fresh receipt preview. Existing previews can retain their older content and show the settings-change warning.
