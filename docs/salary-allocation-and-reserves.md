# Salary allocation and emergency reserves

Salary remains observed income, identified from bank credits by salary wording or the privately configured employer/payee match. Other incoming funds are separate. Future salary is not added to runway.

Finance's collapsed Salary plan uses the latest identified salary date in the last 30 days, combining salary credits on that date. It subtracts external spending since that date, including all same-day spending conservatively, then caps the remaining amount by current bank cash after reserves. Salary already appears in the balance; it is never added again. Transfers and card repayments do not count as external spending, but current balances cap funds actually available.

The plan reserves listed commitments through the day before the next payday, or 30 days if payday is unset, followed by estimated everyday spending. It suggests up to the desired savings amount (or the monthly ISA target when no manual savings target is entered), and shows any additional spending money. Targets that do not fit are shown as shortfalls. Savings already listed as forecast payments should not also be included in the desired savings target. No bank transactions are made.

Emergency reserve settings are in a separate disclosure on Finance and Savings. They are stored privately in existing finance settings, included in private backups, and are not seeded with personal amounts in the public repository. Enter the desired HSBC target, physical cash target, cash currently held and count date, and optional savings contribution target. Targets apply after saving. Unknown cash on hand reserves the whole cash target conservatively. Only a shortfall is reserved once held cash is recorded; cash already withdrawn and counted is not deducted again.

Runway protects the larger of the general buffer and the HSBC reserve target, plus any physical cash top-up. The two bank buffer targets are not added together. Physical emergency cash is displayed separately, not counted as accessible savings or spendable bank cash. Updating a cash count does not create a duplicate savings account valuation.

The salary allocation is withheld for incomplete forecasts, unavailable HSBC balances, missing observed salary or an HSBC balance below target. The app checks the current HSBC balance, but the future forecast pools bank accounts: it cannot guarantee which account an individual bill debits. Users must arrange transfers themselves if necessary. The receipt includes a compact allocation and reserve summary when these settings are configured.
