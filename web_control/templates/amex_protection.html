Statement and Direct Debit settings

Uses the first Amex card returned by your connection. Bank details are captured during finance refreshes. Enable this after checking your statement and payment arrangement.

Automatic mode needs the bank's statement date, full statement balance and due date. Missing fields produce a warning; they are never replaced with the current balance.

Required for a fixed amount. Otherwise leave blank to use the bank's minimum or unpaid statement balance. Confirm the collection amount in Amex after any manual payment.

If blank, the payment due date is used for planning. Check your actual collection date in Amex.

Optional fallback when the feed misses payments. Enter the total credited to this statement, excluding refunds and planned transfers. This is compared with bank-confirmed payments, never added to them. Check or reset it when changing statements.

These controls do not change your Amex Direct Debit or send money. Full statement payment helps avoid purchase interest, subject to your card terms and previous balances; it cannot guarantee that no interest is due.
