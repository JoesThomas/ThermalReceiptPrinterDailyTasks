Tax pot and funding settings

Update rental tax assumptions. The automatic estimate covers the supported rental-tax calculation only. For other liabilities enter a manual total after PAYE/withholding; do not add the rental estimate a second time.

Existing rental-tax money reserved is treated as this same pot: the larger balance is used, not their sum. Update the old reserve when paying tax or changing the pot balance.

If automatic mode leaves this blank, the savings deadline defaults to the supported tax year's end. Check your actual payment deadline with HMRC.

Included cash is protected once as an earmark. External pot money is not deducted from cash again. Unmatched external transfers stay protected until the bank debit is confirmed.

Other annual expenses

Lower priority numbers fund first, after tax. Examples: insurance, MOT costs, Christmas or annual subscriptions. Link the exact bill name (or use the same pot name) to protect an included cash earmark once when that bill is already scheduled. These records fund savings pots; they do not create duplicate bill payments in your forecast.
