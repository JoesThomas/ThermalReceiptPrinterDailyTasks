Overdraft allowances

Overdrafts are borrowing, so they are excluded from cash, runway and savings forecasts. The bank's reported overdraft is deducted automatically. Leave blank to use bank data, or enter the allowance included in that account's available balance. Enter 0 only if its available balance already excludes borrowing.

If the bank omits its overdraft limit, the forecast uses the lower of current and available balances. Enter a limit here to also account for pending payments accurately.
