Therapy payment reminder

When therapy is two or three days away, check recent bank payments before adding “Pay for therapy” to the receipt. Enter the payee as it appears in your bank.

Salary identification

Salary and payroll references are recognised automatically. If your employer appears under a different name in bank transactions, enter that name here so its payments are listed under salary.

Receipt location

The name and region appear in the receipt header and weather. Coordinates choose the weather forecast; the BBC RSS feed chooses the local headlines.

Use a regional feed from feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/. Villa match-day information is controlled separately.

Output

Weather always includes the condition drawing, hourly forecast and temperature graph for your configured location.
