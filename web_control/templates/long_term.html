3. Accounts, debts and assets

A goal is a milestone unless you switch on a contribution stop. Income enters the first bank account; reserve targets redistribute it to other banks at allocation time. The monthly contribution field applies to savings, investments, pensions and debts. Use asset-purchase or withdrawal events for property. Explicit account limits and the Premium Bonds £50,000 cap still apply. Enter property ownership value and mortgage debt separately; pension balances need your chosen access date.

4. Life changes and recurring replacement costs

Salary, other income and spending set a new monthly amount. Income and cost are one-off cash changes. Withdrawals move money from an asset into bank cash, subject to its access date and your flat tax assumption. Contribution changes set an account's monthly contribution. Realise payout explicitly moves separate assumed payout cash into the bank. Asset purchase moves bank cash into an asset at the entered value. Goal tests use the last model point on or before your chosen date; they never borrow a later month’s income. For a vehicle replacement every five years, choose Cost and repeat every 60 months. Future costs increase with the inflation assumption.
