How these lists are calculated

Lists cover planned recipes, including next-day lunch extras, and leave pantry items visible to check. Homemade components may need their own ingredients. Different units stay separate when a recipe gives no conversion.

Shop dates are for grouping purchases. Follow packaging for use-by dates and storage, and freeze suitable portions if needed.

Shopping days & shops

Shopping is scheduled on weekends, with Saturday as the default. Use the same day to combine specialist stops into one outing. Only select a supplier for products it actually stocks. Fresh essentials can go to a local shop instead of another supermarket trip.
