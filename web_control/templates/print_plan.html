Why these sections and reminders appear
  • Information uses your configured location and enabled weather/news sources.
  • Actions include due tasks and today's calendar; put-bin-out reminders appear the day before collection.
  • MOT booking tasks start 30 days before the recorded expiry. Vehicle summaries print on Sundays or at the 30, 14 and 7-day milestones.
  • Birthdays print on the day and in Sunday's next-10-days list. Meals use the current plan when enabled.
  • Selecting Finance here explicitly includes it for this print. Forecast figures remain estimates.

These explanations stay on the web page to avoid repeating them on daily paper.
