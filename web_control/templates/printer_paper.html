Printer size & paper rolls

Roll use estimate

Generate a receipt preview to see yearly roll usage, replacement changes and days per roll. Estimates appear on Receipt preview and Prepare print.

Double Dragon 80 × 80 mm describes paper width and roll diameter, not paper length. The initial 80 m value below is an editable assumption, not a confirmed length for your rolls. Use the length on your packaging or supplier specification.

Printer size

Changing printer dimensions requires restarting Receipt Control. Paper length and usage assumptions update immediately. The printer must support ESC/POS; changing paper size cannot add hardware compatibility.

Custom dimensions and estimate calibration

Custom dimensions apply only with the Custom profile. Use your printer manual's printable width and Font A character width, rather than the roll diameter.
