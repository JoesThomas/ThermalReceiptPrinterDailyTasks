Scope and assumptions

This estimates additional Income Tax caused by rental profit, including Personal Allowance tapering and restricted finance-cost relief. It is not your complete tax return or the exact amount HMRC will collect. Savings interest, dividends, pension/Gift Aid reliefs, special allowances, Scottish tax, companies, overseas property and multiple complex property businesses need a broader calculation.

Capital mortgage repayments are not allowable rental expenses. Unused losses and finance costs need checking before carrying them forward. The cash-flow figure excludes mortgage capital repayments and capital improvements.

A £0 tax estimate does not determine whether a tax return is required. Check the HMRC reporting guidance. Payments on account, tax already paid and filing/payment deadlines are not included. A first Self Assessment bill can require an advance payment as well as the year's balancing payment. Recheck whenever income or tax rules change; this version will stop calculating outside 2026/27.
