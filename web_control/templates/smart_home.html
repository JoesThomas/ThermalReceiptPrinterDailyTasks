Use its fixed private IPv4 address. Credentials are encrypted and never returned in the page. Authentication requirements depend on model and firmware; Tapo control commonly uses the TP-Link account credentials and may require Third-Party Compatibility in Tapo Lab.

Add Yale Smart Alarm

For the newer Yale Smart Alarm with a Global-region Yale Home account (including the UK). This connects to Yale's cloud over verified HTTPS; it is not a local-only alarm connection. Older Sync/Smart Living hubs use a different service and are not supported by this connector.

Account sessions are encrypted with your vault. You may be asked for Yale's email verification code, then to select and verify the alarm area. Every arming/disarming command needs a fresh smart-home authenticator code and a typed confirmation.
